Before you send: the courses are live at https://learn.asejik.com. Check the link opens on your own phone.

## 4. Feedback form: questions

Aim for 3 minutes. Anonymous by default. Mark questions 1, 3 and 7 as required.

**Top of the form (description):**
> Thank you for trying Learn with Asejik. This form is anonymous unless you choose to leave your email at the end. Please do not write passwords, ID numbers or other private details in your answers. By answering you confirm you are 18 or over. Answers are stored in a private Google form that only Asejik can open, and are used only to improve the lessons. To have your answers removed, email privacy@asejik.com.

| # | Question | Type | Options / notes |
| :-- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Which course did you try? (tick all that apply) | Checkboxes, required | Spot Scams Before They Spot You; Use AI Tools Well; How APIs Work |
| 2 | How far did you get? | Multiple choice | Part of one lesson; One whole lesson; Two or three lessons; The whole course |
| 3 | How easy was it to use? | Linear scale 1 to 5, required | 1 = Very hard, 5 = Very easy |
| 4 | What did you use? | Multiple choice | Android phone; iPhone; Computer or tablet. Add a short answer: "Any network problems? (optional)" |
| 5 | Was the site fast and light on your data? | Multiple choice | Yes; Mostly; No; I am not sure |
| 6 | Was anything confusing, broken or hard to tap? Tell me what you saw and where. | Paragraph | Optional |
| 7 | Did you learn something useful? | Multiple choice, required | A lot; Something; Not really |
| 8 | Was anything in the lessons wrong, unclear or too easy or hard? | Paragraph | Optional. Ask them to name the lesson |
| 9 | What would you like to learn next? | Short answer | Optional |
| 10 | How likely are you to recommend it to a friend? | Linear scale 0 to 10 | 0 = Not at all, 10 = Definitely |
| 11 | If you would like a reply, leave your email. Leave it blank to stay anonymous. | Short answer | Optional. Not required |

**How to build it (Google Forms, free):**
1. Go to https://forms.google.com and click **Blank form**.
2. Click the title and type "Learn with Asejik: your feedback". Paste the description above into the description box.
3. For each row above, click **Add question** (the plus icon on the right), type the question, pick the type from the drop-down, and add the options. Switch **Required** on only where marked.
4. Click the **Settings** tab at the top. Under **Responses**, keep **Collect email addresses** set to **Do not collect**, and turn **Limit to 1 response** off (it would force a Google sign-in).
5. Click the **Responses** tab, then the green sheet icon (**Link to Sheets**), then **Create a new spreadsheet**. Keep that sheet private.
6. Click **Send**, then the link icon, tick **Shorten URL**, and click **Copy**. That is the [FEEDBACK FORM LINK] for the messages above.
7. Open the link in a private window on your phone and send yourself a test answer. Delete the test row in the sheet afterwards.

## 5. What to look for when answers come in

- **Question 3 below 4, or any answer to 6:** a usability problem. Note the lesson and screen, and send it to me.
- **Question 8:** possible wrong or unclear content. Send me the lesson name and the sentence.
- **Question 5 "No":** a data or speed problem. Ask which network they used.
- **Question 7 "Not really":** the lesson is not landing. Look at which course they tried.
- Reply personally to anyone who left an email. Do not add their email to the pilot list unless they also used "Join the pilot" on the site (that is the only consent for emailing them about new lessons).
