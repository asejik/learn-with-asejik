## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Learn with Asejik**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our interactive learning platform.

We believe in **privacy by design and data minimization**: we collect only the bare minimum information needed to deliver an interactive, active-recall learning experience.

---

## 2. Who We Are (Data Controller)
Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023), the Data Controller responsible for your personal data is:
* **Controller**: Asejik
* **Website**: [asejik.com](https://asejik.com)
* **Privacy Enquiries**: `privacy@asejik.com`

## 3. The Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us (Optional & Delayed)
You can explore and complete lessons on Learn with Asejik without providing any personal data. When you complete a lesson and choose to save your progress, claim a completion certificate, or join our pilot feedback group, we collect:
* **Full Name**: Used solely to personalize your completion certificate and identify you on our pilot roster.
* **Email Address**: Used solely to send your course certificate, important platform updates, and requests for feedback on your learning experience.

### B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device (IndexedDB)
To provide offline learning and active-recall features without consuming mobile data, our application stores the following on your device's browser IndexedDB:
* Completed lesson IDs and progress percentages.
* Active recall retry queues (questions to review later).
* Thumbs-up / thumbs-down ratings you submit upon lesson completion.
* Consent timestamp and local user profile name/email.

*Note: This local data remains on your phone or computer. We do not automatically upload or sell this data to third parties.*

### C. What We NEVER Collect
* We do **not** collect sensitive personal data (no BVN, NIN, financial data, health data, or biometrics).
* We do **not** track your precise GPS location.
* We do **not** use third-party advertising tracking pixels (no Facebook Pixel, no ad retargeting).
* We do **not** sell or rent your personal data to any third party.

---

## 4. Our Lawful Basis for Processing
Under Section 25 of the NDPA 2023, our lawful basis for collecting and processing your name and email address is **Consent**. 

When you choose to enter your details on the course completion screen, you are presented with an un-ticked checkbox asking for your explicit consent. You have the right to withdraw this consent at any time.

---

## 5. How We Store and Protect Your Data
* **Local Security**: Your progress data is stored in your browser's isolated IndexedDB store, accessible only to the Learn with Asejik web app.
* **Network Security**: All communication between your device and our platform uses secure TLS/HTTPS encryption.
* **Pilot Roster Storage**: When you submit your name and email, the data is transmitted via an encrypted serverless route to a private, access-restricted pilot registry protected by two-factor authentication (2FA).
* **Retention**: We retain pilot learner details for the duration of the pilot phase plus 12 months, or until you request deletion.

---

## 6. Cookies and Local Storage
Learn with Asejik does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We use only **strictly necessary client-side storage** (IndexedDB and Service Worker cache) to:
1. Allow you to take lessons offline without burning mobile data.
2. Remember your progress across browser sessions.

Because these storage mechanisms are strictly necessary to deliver the requested service, no intrusive tracking banner is required.

---

## 7. Your Data Protection Rights (NDPA 2023)
As a learner in Nigeria (and globally), you have clear legal rights regarding your personal data:
1. **Right to Access**: You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
2. **Right to Rectification**: You can ask us to update or correct your name or email address if inaccurate.
3. **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: You can request that we delete your name and email from our pilot roster at any time. You can also wipe your local progress instantly via the "Clear Local Data" button on our platform.
4. **Right to Withdraw Consent**: You may withdraw your consent for email updates at any time by contacting us or clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send.
5. **Right to Lodge a Complaint**: If you believe your data has been mishandled, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** at [ndpc.gov.ng](https://ndpc.gov.ng).
